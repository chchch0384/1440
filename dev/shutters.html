Shutter options

Tap each one to feel the press. Tell me the letter you like.

AiOS camera

The shutter everyone already knows. Instantly readable, the most "camera" of all.

BIndicator light

Keeps the soft raised disc, with one amber light in the middle, like a machine that's ready.

CAmber ring

Just a thin glowing ring. Fills with amber when you press. The quietest option.

DMinute dial

60 ticks around the button, one per minute of this hour. Lit ticks are minutes you own, the current minute blinks. Replaces the "This hour n/60" line.

EKey cap

A solid white key that physically sinks when pressed. Bright and tactile against the dark screen.

FWell

A bowl pressed into the surface with a small button sitting in it. The reverse of the current one.

GMinute face

The button shows the minute you're about to collect. The big clock above could then shrink.
